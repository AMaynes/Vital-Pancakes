PRIVACY BOUNDARY

The movie never leaves your device.

Shared audio is processed in bounded rolling chunks, then discarded. Caption text, compact synchronization data, and projects stay in this browser.

  • No backend, accounts, API keys, analytics, or hidden network transcription.
  • Only pinned model files download from their configured host after you ask.
  • No raw movie audio or video is written to storage or exported.

For lawfully accessed content and personal accessibility. Caption Relay does not bypass DRM or control another website.
