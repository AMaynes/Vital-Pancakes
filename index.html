A living index of remembered knowledge

Somewhere in my synaptic weights...

“Vital Pancakes” (Pinakes Vitae, comically renamed) serves as a collection of research, literature, studies, everyday life, and working tools into a single enduring catalogue that should exist somewhere in my head. It carries forward the spirit of the Pinakes, the lost catalogue by Callimachus (3rd century BCE) that recorded the works of the Library of Alexandria. Here though, the catalogue is personal: it preserves knowledge I have learned and hope not to lose, even as time causes me to forget. If this ever helps anyone in the future, that would also be pretty neat.

Planned features awaiting review and verification. These features are currently unavailable.

Knowledge graph WIP
A visual map will show connections between saved studies, notes, and other resources, with links back to their sources.
Manual linking WIP
Entries will be connectable by hand, with a description of how each pair relates.
AI suggestions WIP
AI will propose possible connections for review before they become accepted links.
Universal search WIP
One search will find saved content across tools, with source links and filters.
Unified vault WIP
A shared backup and restore system will bring locally saved work from across the site into one portable archive.
Vault encryption WIP
Password-based encryption is planned to protect exported vault archives. Its design and recovery limitations still need verification.
Glossary WIP
A central glossary will collect definitions and connect terms to the entries that use them.
Refresh index WIP
An index refresh will update the searchable collection from saved content across tools.
Knowledge analyzer WIP
An analyzer will examine saved knowledge for themes, supporting or conflicting evidence, and possible connections, with source references for review.

Why this exists

“Knowledge survives when it can be found, revisited, and made useful again.”

Vital Pancakes—originally Pinakes Vitae—is a remedy for knowledge lost after the lesson ends: a durable record of learning across disciplines, preserved for return.
