A living index of remembered knowledge

What is learned should not be lost.

“Vital Pancakes” (Pinakes Vitae, comically renamed) gathers research, literature, studies, everyday life, and working tools into a single enduring catalogue—built to recover what time and disuse would otherwise erase. It carries forward the spirit of the Pinakes, the lost catalogue by Callimachus that recorded the works of the Library of Alexandria. Here, the catalogue is personal: it preserves knowledge I have learned and hope not to lose—though forgetting is inevitable, and this is meant to remedy it.

Why this exists

“Knowledge survives when it can be found, revisited, and made useful again.”

Vital Pancakes—originally Pinakes Vitae—is a remedy for knowledge lost after the lesson ends: a durable record of learning across disciplines, preserved for return.
